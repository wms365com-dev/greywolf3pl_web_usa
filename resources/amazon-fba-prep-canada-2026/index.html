Amazon FBA prep in Canada: what changed July 1, 2026

Amazon ended its Canada-store FBA prep and item-labelling services. This guide explains what sellers should organize before inventory reaches an Amazon fulfilment centre.

Use the preparation checklistRead Amazon's announcement

The Canada-store policy change

Amazon announced that, effective July 1, 2026, it would no longer provide prep and item-labelling services for Fulfilment by Amazon shipments in the Canada store. Amazon said products must be prepared and labelled before sellers send them to its facilities.

The announcement applies to inventory moving into Canada FBA, including applicable inventory sent directly or through Amazon supply-chain programs. Amazon also warned that shipments created after July 1 and received without proper preparation or item labels would not be eligible for reimbursement if items became damaged or untraceable.

Primary source: Amazon Seller Central Canada: “Canada FBA prep and labelling services end July 1.” Sellers should always check their current Seller Central workflow because Amazon requirements can change by product and shipment.

A practical FBA preparation checklist

1. Confirm the shipment plan

Identify the ASINs, merchant SKUs, FNSKUs, quantities, destination, unit preparation category, carton configuration and planned ship date. The warehouse needs the approved shipment details before labels or cartons are produced.

2. Control item identification

Provide the correct item-label data and clear instructions for covering or preserving existing barcodes. Test scan quality and maintain a reconciliation between units received, units prepared and units assigned to the shipment.

3. Document product preparation

Confirm whether each product needs a bag, seal, bundle, multipack, protective packaging, warning label or other work. Product-specific requirements should come from the seller's current Amazon workflow, not an old generic checklist.

4. Build compliant cartons and pallets

Define case packs, mixed cartons, carton weights and dimensions, carton-content information, shipping labels and pallet requirements. Do not print final shipping labels before the shipment plan is ready.

5. Keep an exception process

Decide what happens when quantities do not match, barcodes do not scan, packaging is damaged or a product instruction is unclear. Quarantine uncertain units instead of guessing.

6. Retain preparation evidence

Keep receiving counts, work records and shipment reconciliation. If photos are required, define exactly which steps or finished states must be photographed before work begins.

What to ask an FBA prep provider

  • How will the provider receive and validate shipment-plan data?
  • Which item, packaging, carton and pallet tasks can it actually perform?
  • How are quantity differences, scan failures and damaged units reported?
  • What inventory visibility and work-completion records are available?
  • How are Amazon changes communicated and approved before new work begins?
  • Can reserve inventory support later FBA replenishment or DTC fallback orders?
  • What information is required to quote receiving, materials, labour, storage and outbound freight?

Amazon FBA prep questions

When did Amazon Canada end FBA prep and item-labelling services?

Amazon announced that Canada-store FBA prep and item-labelling services ended July 1, 2026.

Who is responsible for preparing Canada FBA inventory now?

The seller must ensure products are prepared and labelled before shipment, either internally or through a qualified provider.
